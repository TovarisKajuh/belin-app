# Demo runbook v2, 12.08.2026

Supersedes `2026-07-20-demo-script.md`, which covered the material-check loop
only. That story is still good and survives here as beats 2 to 4; everything
after it is new.

The product now carries the whole job: the plan goes in, the days get logged,
the things that cost money get caught, and the paperwork comes out signed. The
demo follows that order, because it is the order the customer lives.

---

## Before the meeting

**1. Reset.** `npm run seed`

Puts both demo projects back to their staged state: "Trenutno" two weeks in and
half built, "Dan 1" empty. The reset also clears anything a previous demo
finished: the handover request, the acceptance, the invoice and any completion
report, including the PDFs in storage. The daily entries and material checks are
NOT cleared, because they are the live feed the dashboard opens on.

**2. Check what the seed staged for you.** It already stages the commercial
state, so there are no preparation clicks left:

- Trenutno: an ACCEPTED naročilnica with a price and an hourly rate, one
  approved hour sheet, a second sheet with two working days left on its
  countdown, an approved extra, two incidents, one answered request and one
  open, and the subcontractor's compliance documents including one expiring in
  eighteen days.
- Dan 1: a SENT naročilnica waiting to be accepted.

Both naročilnica PDFs are real documents rendered through the app's own code
with real hashes, so accepting one on stage passes the hash check.

**3. Two devices, or two browser tabs.**

- EPC (laptop): `12345` / `12345`
- Crew (phone): `54321` / `54321`

The bottom-left pill switches between Trenutno and Dan 1 without logging out.

The password login exists only where `DEMO_LOGIN=1`, which is set locally and in
no Vercel environment. On production, sign in by email link.

## The story, beat by beat

### 1. The plan goes in (Dan 1)

On the laptop, "Nov projekt". Drop a real K2 report onto the first step. Watch
the name, the address, the power, the roofs, the module count and the material
list fill themselves in. Change one quantity to show it is all editable, attach
the subcontractor, commit.

NARRATION: "You did not type a project. You reviewed one."

### 2. Day one on the roof (Dan 1)

Switch the phone to Dan 1. The first time a phone opens the link it asks who is
holding it: tap a name from the crew, and that is the last time that phone ever
sees the link. From then on Belin opens signed in, from the home screen. Say
this out loud, because it is the answer to "so my guys have to log in every
morning?" and the answer is no, once, ever.

The crew cannot file a daily report yet: the screen asks them to check the
material first. Nothing gets logged until we know what arrived.

Tap "Vse prispelo", then mark one line "Delno" and type how much is missing.
Attach a photo of the pallet and one of the dobavnica. Send. Under half a minute
of tapping, one thumb.

### 3. The other side sees it (Dan 1)

On the laptop the material panel shows the shortfall, both documents and the
exact time of the check. No phone call. If the crew submits a report right now,
the dashboard updates on its own within a couple of seconds.

Add a part to the list from the EPC side; the crew's phone is immediately asked
to check the new part; they check it; the panel settles.

### 4. The QR moment (optional, strong)

Show the crew link as a QR from Settings and let the prospect scan it with their
own phone. They are on the site in one scan, no account, no password, and their
report appears on the wall dashboard while everyone is watching.

**SECURITY RITUAL:** a crew token shown to a room is a live capability, though a
smaller one than it used to be: it now only lets somebody claim a name, and the
claim shows up on the boss's roster in Settings where he can remove it. After
any event where the QR was displayed, still regenerate that project's crew token
(delete and re-insert the `project_tokens` row) and check the roster for names
you do not recognise. The four demo tokens are stable by design; a token on a
wall is not covered by that rule.

### 5. Mid-project (switch both to Trenutno)

The same screens, two weeks of real data. Progress, tempo, the day list with
photos and weather.

- **An incident:** the crew files a rain stop with a photo in seconds. The EPC
  sees it live and gets an email.
- **Regiestunden:** open the hour sheet with two working days left. The
  countdown is visible to both sides. NARRATION: "If nobody reacts, it counts as
  approved after six working days, and both sides knew that in advance." Approve
  it; the subcontractor is notified.
- **A change order:** approve the extra. It now has a price and will appear on
  the invoice by itself.

### 6. The paperwork (Trenutno)

This is the close.

- As the subcontractor, "Zaključi projekt". The EPC is told the job is ready for
  handover.
- As the EPC, "Ustvari poročilo": a completion report with a cover, one numbered
  page per day with weather, photos and quantities, and registers of hours,
  extras and incidents. Twelve pages, generated in a few seconds.
- "Začni prevzem": fill the attendees, add one defect with a deadline, choose
  "Prevzeto s pridržki", tick the penalty reservation, and take both signatures
  on the screen, passing the device across the table. The protocol prints the
  reservation sentence verbatim, which is the sentence that keeps the right
  alive.
- As the subcontractor, "Ustvari račun". It assembles itself from the accepted
  naročilnica, the approved hours and the approved extra. On a domestic
  Slovenian construction job it prints the reverse-charge note and no VAT line
  at all.

NARRATION: "Nobody typed any of this. It is the same data the crew entered on
the roof."

### 7. Zoom out

Back to the project list: every project with its progress, status and the
aggregate numbers across the portfolio.

## Rough edges to steer around

- The completion report takes a few seconds on the first generation of a
  session. Click it before you talk about it.
- Landing page screenshots are not in yet; those sections render as framed
  captioned panels. Scroll past, or capture them from this demo first.
- The German and English catalogs are complete, but the product has been driven
  hardest in Slovenian. If demoing in German, walk it once beforehand.
- One project cannot be demoed twice through beat 6 without re-seeding: the
  handover, acceptance and invoice are once-only by design.

## Reset between meetings

`npm run seed` again. It is rerunnable and idempotent, and it clears the closing
documents so the next demo starts from the same clean state.
