## Global constraints

- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, including captions, alt texts, video cards, legal pages.
- Slovenian-first: every new UI string is real Slovenian; de and en carry the Slovenian string until the deliberate translation pass (thread D, item 1) covers them.
- Every produced image gets LOOKED AT before it is called done, at native size on the dark ground, not only at display size.
- One database serves local and production: any pipeline run seeds before and re-seeds after.
- The report path stays under 30 seconds, one hand, on a roof. Any change that adds a tap to it is wrong by definition.

## Review lenses (apply to every thread before calling it done)

1. **Roofer's thumb:** reachable one-handed at 375px, targets 44px+, nothing added to the report path.
2. **Prospect's eye:** does the artifact SELL: signed evidence, money language, zero lorem anywhere.
3. **Rural LTE:** weight budget respected: WebP, no autoplaying video on the landing, measured page weight.
4. **Legal and trust:** demo names only in marketing assets, Impressum and privacy exist before German eyes, no live credentials anywhere.

## Deemed decisions (founder veto list)

1. **The documents are captured born-transparent** via the same CSS 3D + `omitBackground` path as the device mockups. No background removal happens because no background ever exists. The flood-fill and checkerboard tools stay for external exports only.
2. **Composition:** three sheets on one desk, mirror-symmetric angles composing toward the center, small overlap on desktop. Chronological order (poročilo, zapisnik, račun) puts the **zapisnik with its two visible signatures in the center star position**. Signed paper is the strongest single image this product can show.
3. **Uniform crop at 65% of page height** with a bottom fade on all three. 65% is measured, not chosen by eye: it keeps the report's stat tiles and summary, the zapisnik's defect row, penalty box and both signatures (~58% height), and the invoice's line items, Za plačilo and the 76.a clause (~48%).
4. **Tabs ship on the roof surfaces only.** SubHome (office view) keeps its command bar; the boss on the roof reaches the tabs through the same crew screens he already opens. EPC surfaces untouched.
5. **Tab set: Pregled, Poročaj (center, camera, default), Dnevnik, plus Ure gated by the capability the hours page already checks.** No fifth tab. No profile tab: language lives in the command bar, logout in the pill.
6. **Video tooling: Playwright takes + ffmpeg-static. Remotion rejected for v1**: a second rendering pipeline is not worth it for captions-only. Captions are sharp-rendered PNG overlays, not drawtext, so typography matches the app.
7. **No video embedded on the landing page v1.** It ships to LinkedIn and WhatsApp; the landing stays light for LTE.

## Thread A: Section 04, the three documents

**What exists:** `assets/marketing/docs/completion-cover.png`, `abnahme.png`, `invoice.png`, 1786x2526 rasters of the real PDFs, regenerated by `marketing:pdfs`. Section 04 in `Story.tsx` renders three `Shot` slots at ratio 1/1.414 pointing at files that do not exist, so framed caption panels show.

**Harmony rules, stated as law:**
1. One scale: all three sheets render at identical width, so the shared BELIN header lockup and type sizes align across the row.
2. One crop: top 65% of the page, applied with sharp BEFORE the 3D capture. Bottom 10% of each sheet fades to transparent via a CSS mask inside the capture page, so the fade follows the rotated edge, not the frame.
3. One angle family: left `rotateY(10deg) rotateZ(-1.2deg)`, center `rotateX(3deg)` at scale 1.06, right `rotateY(-10deg) rotateZ(1.2deg)`. Same perspective (1200px) on all three.
4. One light: the identical two-layer drop-shadow filter the device mockups use, direction down-right.

**Files:**
- Modify: `scripts/marketing/mockups.mjs` (a `paper` device: white sheet, 2px radius, crop + fade mask + 3D capture; three jobs)
- Modify: `components/landing/Story.tsx` (section 04 media becomes `lp-papers`, plain `Image`s, captions demoted to alt)
- Modify: `app/globals.css` (`.lp-papers`: desktop three across bottom-aligned with ~6% overlap, center on top; phone: horizontal scroll-snap row, sheets at 72vw)
- Output: masters in `assets/marketing/mockups/doc-*.png`, serving copies `public/landing/doc-{report,abnahme,invoice}.webp` at ~900px width

**Steps:**
- [ ] **Step 1:** Implement the `paper` capture. Run. READ all three masters at native size on the dark ground; check the crop kept the signatures and the 76.a clause, the fades sit at one height, the shadows agree.

## Thread B: The roof tab bar

**Ground truth:** `CrewHome` is one scroll column: progress hero, quick buttons (incident, re-check), material gate, report form with a fixed submit bar, today's feed. The report form being immediately present IS the 30-second law in action.

**Design:** the primary action keeps the primary position. Cold open lands on **Poročaj**, which is today's CrewHome minus the feed: material gate, form, quick buttons, fixed submit above the tab bar with a measured 12px gap. **Pregled** takes the read parts: progress hero, material status, today's feed. **Dnevnik** is new but read-only: past days, what was filed, by whom. **Ure** links the existing hours surface, shown only when the capability check the hours page already performs passes. Tab bar: fixed bottom, 64px row, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, camera as a 56px raised gold circle, active state gold, phone widths only.

**Architecture:** tabs are LINKS to routes, not client state: `/app/[id]` (Poročaj, default), `/app/[id]/pregled`, `/app/[id]/dnevnik`, `/app/[id]/hours` (exists). Server components, back button works, deep links work, Next prefetch hides LTE latency.

**Files:**
- Create: `components/crew/CrewTabs.tsx`, `app/[locale]/app/[projectId]/pregled/page.tsx`, `.../dnevnik/page.tsx`
- Modify: `CrewHome` split, `app/globals.css`, `messages/*.json` (`crew.tabs.*`, Slovenian, placeholders de/en)

**Steps:**
- [ ] **Step 1:** Data first: what Dnevnik lists exists in the reports data layer; write the read query and its test before any UI.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Build the bar and routes. The report path regression check is the acceptance test: cold open to submitted report in under 30 seconds, counted taps unchanged from today.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Verify at 375 on the real phone viewport: submit bar above tab bar, no overlap, InstallHint does not collide, safe area respected.
