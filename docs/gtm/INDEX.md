## The three epistemic tiers

Every claim in this base is one of three kinds, and they have different rules.

| Tier | What it is | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **FACT** | Verifiable, and expensive to get wrong: prices, tool capabilities, market numbers, dates, legal constraints | Verified before asserted. Source link and access date recorded. |
| **PATTERN** | Synthesized regularity from multiple inputs: how buyers behave, what formats work | Inputs named once, then used freely. |
| **J** | Judgment. Instinct, which is compressed experience | Marked J on first use. Never blocked for lacking a source. Every judgment that becomes advice gets its outcome recorded in `field-log.md`. Judgment earns rank by being right and loses it by being wrong. |

This replaces an earlier "every claim needs a source" rule, which was founder-corrected: experts run on calibrated instinct, and the honest way to make instinct trustworthy is to keep score of it, not to ban it.

## The one finding that outranks the rest so far

**The residential solar collapse is not Belin's collapse.** Slovenia 2025: residential down about 77 percent, commercial and industrial down only 8 percent. Austria: the decline is explicitly in sub-20 kW residential. Germany: megawatts roughly flat while the number of installing companies falls, meaning work is consolidating into fewer, larger firms.

Belin serves commercial roofs with subcontracted montage. That segment held. And companies fleeing collapsed residential are arriving in it with no process for subcontracted work, no acceptance discipline and no hour-sheet control. **That migration, not the solar boom, is the market Belin sells into.** Detail in `market-icp-si.md` section 2.

## Standing constraints inherited from the product side

- **The demo seed names a real ZSFV member company** (Sonce Energija d.o.o.) as its EPC. Must be renamed before any public asset shows it.
- **German outreach is gated** on Impressum, privacy policy, and mail deliverability. One warm German pilot is fine; a German campaign is not.
- **The login screen is a dead end for strangers**: there is no self-serve signup, so any public call to action must route to a person, not to the app.
