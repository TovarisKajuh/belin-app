## Summary

Belin produces six document types: Naročilnica/Bestellung, Regiebericht, Bautagesbericht (only inside the completion report), Abschlussbericht, Abnahmeprotokoll, Rechnung. They are clean, consistently styled, fully translated (no Slovenian leaks in de/en, all diacritics č š ž ć đ ä ö ü ß render, text layer extracts correctly) and the reverse charge logic is right for SI, DE and AT. But tomorrow they would embarrass in four ways a German or Austrian Bauleiter spots in seconds: (1) the database is paused, so no document can be opened or generated at all; (2) the staged data prints contradictions (naročilnica accepted 10 days before it was issued; Abnahme warranty starting 18 days before the Abnahme; a German GmbH with a Slovenian VAT id; no addresses anywhere); (3) the invoice is missing statutory fields (addresses, service period, due date) so it is not a valid invoice under § 14 Abs. 4 UStG or 82. člen ZDDV-1; (4) layout bugs on every page with real crew text: paragraphs render double spaced, day pages do not name the project, portrait photos are cropped to strips, and signature lines orphan onto empty pages. Legally, the one statement that is wrong rather than incomplete is the Regiestunden deemed approval for Austrian sites: Belin applies 6 Werktage everywhere, ÖNORM B 2110 says 2 weeks.

Gap side: the workflow and Salzburg materials promise documents that do not exist (Strangmessprotokoll, Sicherheitsunterweisung, Ponudba, Pooblastilo, Vloga, Povpraševanje, single-line diagram). The cheapest high-value additions on top of existing data are a Nachtrag PDF, a Behinderungsanzeige from obstruction incidents, a single-day Bautagesbericht download, a weekly report, and an acceptance certificate for the Bestellung.
